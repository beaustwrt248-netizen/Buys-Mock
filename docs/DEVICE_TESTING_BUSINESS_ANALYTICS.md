# Device Testing and Business Analytics

This slice adds two Admin intelligence surfaces without creating CRM or inventory-lifecycle functionality.

## Device Testing

Staff can explicitly record Display, Touch, Camera, Speaker, Microphone, Charging, Buttons, Vibration and Connectivity as pass, fail or unsupported. Untested checks remain unavailable. No check is marked automated unless the platform has independently verified that automation path; this UI intentionally records staff verification only.

## Business Analytics

Operational metrics are derived only from evidence already present in the Morley Intelligence runtime: catalogue records/completeness, authoritative buy-price coverage, marketplace exceptions, active alerts, support pressure and release risks. Missing source datasets return unavailable values rather than zero or estimates.

Customer profiles, CRM, stock lifecycle, stocktake, shelf/bin management and replacement inventory intelligence remain outside scope.
